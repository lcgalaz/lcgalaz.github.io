<div class="prose" markdown="1">

I am a **Research Economist at the Mexican central bank** ([Banco de México](https://www.banxico.org.mx/){:target="_blank"}), currently working in the Macroprudential Policy Division and previously at the Board of Governors, where I worked with the former Deputy Governor Gerardo Esquivel. I completed my **master's degree in Economics** at [El Colegio de México](https://www.colmex.mx/){:target="_blank"} (ColMex) and my **undergraduate studies in Economics** at the [Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México](https://www.economia.unam.mx/){:target="_blank"} (UNAM).

I am interested in **macroeconomics, financial stability, and financial history**. In particular, I want to understand how crises build up and propagate, and how policy frameworks designed for advanced economies travel to places like Mexico.

**I am applying to PhD programs in Economics for the 2027 entry cycle.** For that purpose, I was awarded the **Fulbright–García Robles Fellowship** for doctoral studies in the United States.
