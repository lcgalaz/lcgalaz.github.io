I am a Research Economist at [Banco de México](https://www.banxico.org.mx/){:target="_blank"} (Mexico's central bank), currently in the Macroprudential Policy Division and previously at the Board of Governors, working with former Deputy Governor Gerardo Esquivel. I completed my master's degree in Economics at [El Colegio de México](https://www.colmex.mx/){:target="_blank"} and my undergraduate studies in Economics at the [Faculty of Economics, UNAM](https://www.economia.unam.mx/){:target="_blank"}.

**I am applying to PhD programs in Economics for the 2027 entry cycle.**

<a class="cv-btn" href="{{ '/documents/CV_Camila_Galaz.pdf' | relative_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Download CV (PDF)</a>

Most of what interests me lies between macroeconomics and financial stability. I want to understand how crises build up, how they propagate, and how policy frameworks designed for advanced economies travel to places like Mexico. I approach these questions mostly empirically. A parallel strand of my work engages economic and financial history, an interest I have long held.
